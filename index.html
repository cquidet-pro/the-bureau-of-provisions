We’re designing a bottle to accompany the everyday: the commute, the café, the walk that becomes an afternoon. Thoughtful drinking details, a carry strap, and just enough character to make it yours.

01 / The bottle

A little more resourceful.

Two ways to sip.
One way to cool the afternoon.

A proper drink.

A broad stainless-steel spout with a softly rounded lip. For a well-earned pause.

The chug spout
II

A leisurely sip.

A slender, curved stainless mouthpiece that folds under the clear hood. Sip, then carry on.

The folding straw

A welcome mist.
